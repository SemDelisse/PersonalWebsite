My projects

These are some projects I made alone or together with other people. Some of the projects are still in development, but you can take a look at what I have made so far if you want!

Games

Puzzle Platformer

One of my own first games that I have created. Puzzle Platformer is a 2D-Platformer game with a simple mechanic system and a story. Available to download and play for Windows and Mac through GitHub. Have fun!

We Are All Missing

With a great team I am working on a 3D puzzle horror game in the game engine Unity. You can see how the project has turned out in the video linked underneath.

Zombies

A 2D topdown roguelike shooter game that I am making on my own. In this game you need to protect yourself by upgrading weapons and defences. You can unlock more maps, weapons, upgrades and research to make your game easier or harder.

Tanks

This is a recreation of the old Wii minigame named Tanks. In this game you have to complete levels by shooting the enemy tanks and avoid getting shot yourself. Complete all levels to win!

Little Shop

A tiny shop game where you have to fill up shelves with products you receive daily while customers buy it up.

Point and click adventure

This is a simple point and click adventure game that you can play at a website. It is made using HTML, CSS and JS and is a short story adventure game that follows the life of a simple player that only has one mission. Getting in the tent!
